Design philosophy

These skills are built to make Claude operate as a specialized expert rather than a generic chatbot — the way a good library doesn't hand you every book at once, it hands you the right one.

Each entry emphasizes structured workflows, reusable systems, verification, quality control, explicit assumptions, and honest handling of uncertainty. Self-contained, and readable in the time it takes to open a folder.

Pulling a card from the drawer

Every skill is self-contained — one folder, one SKILL.md, ready to load into Claude's Skills interface.

Clone or download claude-skills from GitHub.
Open the folder for the specialization you need — say, figma-expert/.
Add its SKILL.md to Claude's Skills interface.
For connector-dependent skills (Figma Expert, and similar), make sure the matching tool or connector is available to Claude.
